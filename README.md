## 🌅 The Premise

> *She didn't ask to be chosen. The blade didn't ask for permission.*

In a world where empires are built on stolen souls, the **Dominion** raids a temple and takes a sacred relic. The warrior **Itzil** — who never wanted the role and doesn't believe the prophecy — discovers she can manifest a weapon of golden light that has not appeared in **three hundred years**.

The Dominion is hunting **seven ancient relics**. Together they open a Gate sealed for millennia. On the other side is something *vast, hungry, and patient*.

Itzil must gather allies, reach the relics first, and become the leader the world needs — while remaining entirely unconvinced she is the leader anyone wants.

**The central thesis of the series:** the most powerful weapon isn't the sharpest sword. It's the person holding it.

## 🎭 The Three-Act Arc

The twelve books are built as three deliberate movements. This is not a monster-of-the-week series — the emotional register of each act is different by design.

| Act | Books | Title | The Turn |
|:---:|:-----:|:------|:---------|
| **I** | 1–4 | **The Rising** | Discovery, alliance, and the death of optimism |
| **II** | 5–8 | **The Breaking** | Everything the alliance built comes apart |
| **III** | 9–12 | **The Reckoning** | Rebuilt from pieces, and what comes after victory |

<details>
<summary><b>Act I — The Rising</b> (Books 1–4) · <i>click to expand</i></summary>

<br>

Hope, assembly, and the slow realization of scale. The act ends when optimism dies and is replaced by something harder.

- **Book 1 · Sunblade Rising** — A reluctant warrior discovers her impossible gift and launches a desperate mission behind enemy lines with a team of broken, brilliant strangers.
- **Book 2 · The Mirror Siege** — Mirror-portals open across the continent. A lone scout goes behind enemy lines to destroy the source. What he finds is worse than a weapon — it's a prison built from a human soul.
- **Book 3 · Ash Oaths** — The war moves from battlefields to ballrooms. A diplomat faces the Dominion's most dangerous negotiator, and the true nature of ash-oaths is decoded: they don't just enslave, they *fuel the Gate*.
- **Book 4 · The Starless Crown** — A ring of void appears in the sky. The real enemy reveals itself. The optimism dies. What replaces it is resolve: hard, cold, unbreakable.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Act II — The Breaking</b> (Books 5–8) · <i>click to expand</i></summary>

<br>

The alliance is dismantled — strategically, physically, and emotionally. This is the act that earns the ending.

- **Book 5 · The Serpent's Gambit** — The Architect arrives: smarter, more patient, more terrifying than anything faced so far. He doesn't fight wars. He *engineers* them.
- **Book 6 · Rain of Obsidian** — Obsidian shards fall from the sky. The base is destroyed. The alliance is scattered. The darkest book in the series, and the one that proves what these characters are made of.
- **Book 7 · Feathers and Bone** — A shadow master returns to the city she abandoned. A healer frees hundreds. The sacrifice demanded in this book will break your heart.
- **Book 8 · The Shattered Blade** — The blade shatters. The mentor dies. Everything breaks so that everything can be rebuilt. The girl who held the sun discovers what she is without it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Act III — The Reckoning</b> (Books 9–12) · <i>click to expand</i></summary>

<br>

Reassembly, reckoning, and a deliberate refusal to end on an explosion.

- **Book 9 · The Forge of Souls** — Nine heroes give a piece of their soul to reforge the blade. The quiet man no one watched is about to save the world.
- **Book 10 · The Mirror Queen** — A healer walks into a dimension of shattered glass where every reflection hides an assassin. Compassion becomes a weapon of mass liberation.
- **Book 11 · Crown of Stars** — Every villain falls. The Gate closes. The Crown shatters into a million points of golden light. Dawn breaks — real dawn.
- **Book 12 · The Sun That Never Sets** — The war is over. The world is saved. Now comes the harder part: learning to live in the world you saved. *The ending is not an explosion. It's a sunrise.*

</details>

## 🛡️ The Alliance

Itzil does not fight alone. The core team assembled in Book 1 carries the emotional weight of all twelve:

| Role | Arc |
|:-----|:----|
| **Itzil** — the sunblade bearer | A reluctant warrior who must become a leader she doesn't believe in |
| **The scout** — bitter, solitary | Goes behind enemy lines in Book 2 and finds a prison built from a soul |
| **The duelist** — flashy, showy | Style as armor over something more fragile |
| **The engineer** — quiet, unwatched | *The quiet man no one watched is about to save the world* (Book 9) |
| **The healer** — impossible abilities | Compassion weaponized into mass liberation (Books 7, 10) |

The full cast runs to **30 distinct characters**, each with consistent visual reference art generated for the series.

**Antagonists:** the **Dominion**, and — from Book 5 — **The Architect**, who engineers wars rather than fighting them.

## 🏭 The Production Pipeline

Each book moves through the same repeatable stages, which is why twelve of them exist:

```
   Concept  ──▶  Series Bible  ──▶  Chapter Outline  ──▶  Manuscript
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
   Retail Listing  ◀──  Cover Art  ◀──  Image Prompts  ◀──  Front/Back Matter
        │
        ▼
   EPUB + Print PDF  ──▶  Amazon KDP
```

1. **Series Bible** — characters, factions, and the full book arc, locked before drafting *(see the series bible PDF at the repo root)*
2. **Chapter Outline** — 24 beats per book, held in `chapter-outlines/`
3. **Manuscript** — drafted into `book-series/`, with series-specific front and back matter
4. **Image Prompts** — engineered in `book-prompts/`, tuned for character consistency across 30 characters and 12 covers
5. **Art Generation** — output collected in `book-images/`
6. **Retail Assets** — titles, blurbs, descriptions, categories, and keywords in `amazon-production/`
7. **Builds** — EPUB and print-ready PDF, one of each per book

## 🌐 The Website

A **Next.js 16** App Router site lives in `src/`, presenting the series to readers.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev     # http://localhost:3000
```

```bash
npm run build   # production build
npm start       # serve the production build
```

**Stack:** Next.js 16.1.6 · React 19.2.3 · TypeScript 5 (strict) · Tailwind CSS 4 · Lucide React · deployed on Vercel
